## What it is

Open it, log the set, put the phone down. Next time Overload shows you what you lifted, so you know what to beat.

## Why I built it

Every gym app I tried wanted a subscription and made me dig through menus to log a single set.

So I built my own. Two weeks later it was on the App Store, free.

## Features

- Progression history for every exercise
- Charts of your trend over time
- Rest timer per exercise
- Volume calculation
- About 800 exercises
- Apple Health integration
