# cavallinilorenzo.com

Lorenzo Cavallini's personal portfolio site and the family of project sites that hang off it.

## Language

### Projects

**Project**:
One of Lorenzo's own products shown on the portfolio (Overload, Oxide, Clicky.rs, GNAM!).
_Avoid_: Startup, app, product

**Project card**:
The tile that represents a Project in the portfolio's project grid.
_Avoid_: Tile, project preview

**Project page**:
A page inside the portfolio dedicated to a single Project.
_Avoid_: Case study, detail page

**Project landing**:
A separate site for a single Project, in that Project's own brand, served on its own subdomain of the portfolio domain.
_Avoid_: Microsite, product site, landing page

**Project status**:
The public lifecycle state of a Project: Pre-release, On the App Store, Live or Closed.
_Avoid_: State, stage

### Experience

**Timeline entry**:
One period or single moment on the Experience timeline: education, work, a hackathon or a Project.
_Avoid_: Event, milestone, item

**Ongoing**:
A Timeline entry with no end yet; the only thing the accent colour marks on the Experience page.
_Avoid_: Current, active
